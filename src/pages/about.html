About

A window company for this coastline.

Sunset Coast Windows replaces windows and patio doors for homes across greater Los Angeles. We are a new company. The work is straightforward: the right vinyl line, an honest installed price, and a crew that leaves the stucco looking the way we found it.

What we actually do

Most of the houses we see still have their original aluminum sliders. They fog, they stick, and they leak air when the marine layer sits on the west side. We replace them with vinyl windows ordered through Beacon Building Products, now part of QXO, including lines from Simonton, Ply Gem, MI Windows, Paradigm, and Atrium.

You can price the project online. If the tape measure makes you nervous, book a visit. The number you saw stays the starting point, and the final quote is written after we measure.

  • Low-E glass as the baseline, with tempered glass where the opening needs it.
  • Retrofit inserts for stucco, and full-frame when the old frame is done.
  • A written workmanship warranty on the install, plus the manufacturer’s warranty on the window.
