Trust & warranty

Stucco-friendly installs, in writing.

  • Most greater-LA replacements are retrofit inserts, so the stucco stays put. Full-frame is an option when the frame is rotten or the opening is being rebuilt.
  • Dual-pane Low-E glass is included. Solar-control, tempered, obscure, and triple-pane are options on the lines that offer them.
  • Manufacturer warranties follow the brand. Many of these vinyl lines carry a limited lifetime warranty for the original homeowner. Read that warranty before you count on it.
  • Every install includes our written workmanship warranty. The term is on your contract.
  • We can coordinate the permit. Old-window removal and disposal is optional on the estimate.

FAQ

Before you book.

Does the price include installation?

Yes. Each window shows a material price and an installation price. Installation is based on the size of the opening. Retrofit, or insert, installation is the default. Full-frame, stucco patching, removal, and permits are listed separately if you add them.

What if I don’t know the size?

Use the measurement helper: width at the top, middle, and bottom, height at the left, center, and right, and we keep the smallest. Or choose “Not sure, measure for me.” You’ll still see a planning price, and the final quote is confirmed after we measure.

Is the online number a contract?

No. It is an installed estimate from the sizes and options you enter. When you book a visit, we keep that price as the starting point and confirm the final quote on site. Ordering online does not charge a card. Payment is not connected yet.

Where do the windows come from?

We order through Beacon Building Products, now part of QXO. The catalog follows lines Beacon shows publicly, including Simonton, Ply Gem, MI Windows, Paradigm, and Atrium. A few shapes are the same brand families and are confirmed with the branch before they are ordered.

Will you work on stucco?

Yes. A retrofit insert is the usual approach in stucco and leaves the wall finish alone. If the old frame has to come out, choose full-frame and, if the stucco needs a patch, add that on the estimate.

How long does a replacement take?

A few openings are often finished in a day. A whole house is usually scheduled across one to three days, depending on count, access, and whether any openings are full-frame. We’ll confirm the schedule with the order.
